# Paper-trading candle and entry policy

Paper trading uses validated, chronological OHLCV bars. Invalid bars are rejected, the first valid provider row for a duplicate timestamp is retained, and bars whose timeframe has not fully completed are excluded before indicator enrichment. New entries carry `fillModelVersion: paper-first-post-signal-close-v1`. Records created by earlier versions retain their original version, and records without a version remain legacy/unknown; they are never relabeled to imply a different entry assumption.

Historical bars provide indicator warm-up and recovery context. A newly started service or observer first records the latest completed candle as its baseline and does not create entries from older signals. A new entry is considered only when the prior processed candle is immediately adjacent to the newly completed candle. If the observer was down for one or more candles, those missed entry opportunities are skipped. Existing open trades continue to be evaluated on available candles after their last recorded evaluation timestamp; their holding period advances only for newly evaluated bars.

The signal timestamp marks the candle whose completed data produced the signal. The service or observer detects an eligible signal only after fetching completed bars. It records that detection time and does not use an open or close that was already timestamped at or before detection. If the just-completed bar is not strictly later than detection, the entry is deferred until the next adjacent completed bar. The modeled entry price is that later bar's close, and the entry time is the end of that bar's interval. The entry bar's earlier high and low are not used to manage the new trade, because the modeled entry occurs at its close. Pending entry opportunities exist only in process memory; a restart or missed bar drops them rather than replaying a stale price.

This is a delayed historical OHLC approximation, not a true real-time first-available price. OHLC data cannot reveal the first quote after signal detection, the bid/ask spread, or whether a broker could execute at the bar close. A close timestamp later than detection establishes only that the recorded bar interval ended later; it does not prove an executable fill or live-trading performance. The dashboard identifies new entries with `paper-first-post-signal-close-v1`. Older `paper-ohlc-gap-aware-v1` entries used the prior bar-open assumption, while entries with no version are Legacy / unknown. Existing trade records are not rewritten.

The dashboard service retains its forward-only cursor in memory; a service restart establishes a new baseline and skips missed entries. The observer persists its per-symbol candle cursor. The paper engine currently supports long, qualified scanner setups only. A gap opening at or below the active stop exits at the opening price. A gap opening at or above the target exits at the target price without assumed price improvement. If the open is between the barriers and one candle touches both, the stop-first policy is retained. These exit rules remain OHLC-based simulation assumptions, not executable bid/ask fills. Existing closed trade records are not recalculated.
